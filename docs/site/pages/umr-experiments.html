UMR Experiments

Establish correspondence first, then evaluate kinematic retargeting.

Approved study guideline

Two stages, different evidence.

UMR learns surface correspondence from static source and robot point clouds, then binds slots to meshes. Its kinematic stage follows those bindings through per-frame optimization and trajectory filtering. Good surface fit alone does not establish anatomical correspondence or physically feasible motion.

Stage 1

Correspondence → bindings

Freeze actor, geometry, normalization and seeds. Train fresh slots; inspect anatomical-zone assignments, coverage and projection.

Kinematic retargeting
